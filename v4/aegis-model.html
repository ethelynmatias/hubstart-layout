The Foundation

Your Central Routing Engine

The heart of a modern agency is its Routing Engine. Instead of tasks landing randomly in inboxes, every piece of work—from a form submission, to a phone call, to an email—is systematically handled.

  • Intake. All work enters a single, structured system.
  • Classification. Each task is instantly categorized into one of 22 specific Task Buckets (e.g., COI Issuance, New Business Intake, Claims Follow-up).
  • Routing. The task is automatically assigned to the most efficient handler based on pre-defined logic.
