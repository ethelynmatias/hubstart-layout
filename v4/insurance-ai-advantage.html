The Difference

What is an "Agentic AI"?

An Agentic AI is an intelligent system that doesn't wait to be told what to do. It can initiate tasks, propose actions, and escalate items based on data, triggers, and timelines without being prompted by a human.

  • 24/7 Orchestration. AI Agents work around the clock, initiating workflows and tasks without human intervention.
  • Learning and Improving. Agents learn from patterns and optimize workflows over time.
  • Working with Experts (Human in the loop). When needed, agents escalate to human experts for decision-making.

A Bot issues a COI when you click a button. An AI Agent notices a contract is expiring and initiates the renewal process 90 days in advance, assigning the first task to a VE.
